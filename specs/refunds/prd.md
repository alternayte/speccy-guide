# Refunds

## Problem

Support staff refund card payments by hand in the provider console. In October, 410 refunds
took a mean of 9 minutes each, and 3% went to the wrong order.

## Users

Support staff who answer refund requests.

## Goals

- **G-1:** A refund takes under 1 minute by the end of Q1.

## Non-goals

- Refunds for bank transfers.

## Requirements

- **REQ-001:** The system must refund a card payment from the order screen.
- **REQ-002:** The system MUST refund a part of an order.
- **REQ-003:** The system SHOULD notify the customer quickly.

## Success metrics

- Mean refund time, from the support tool's audit log.
